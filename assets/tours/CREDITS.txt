EVA desktop tours / Recorridos de escritorio EVA
Recorded from the EVA source desktop in a disposable test session on 2026-11-06 (America/Santiago).
Each language uses one continuous screen recording. Encoding, narration and subtitles do not change the order or speed of the desktop footage.
Demonstration files and a private profile are used; no personal documents, API credentials or real conversations are recorded.
Hardware readings are the host machine's live readings, not benchmark results.
Local video playback is routed to MPRIS through a small test adapter; no game execution or AI response is simulated.

Free production tools: wlroots / EVA, Quickshell, grim, wf-recorder, FFmpeg, Piper.
Synthetic narration, generated locally with Piper (https://github.com/OHF-Voice/piper1-gpl).
English voice: en_US-ljspeech-medium, based on LJ Speech (public-domain dataset).
Spanish voice: es_MX-ald-medium, based on Ald Mexican Spanish (Unlicense dataset).
Models: https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices
The voice models and development tools are not bundled in the EVA ISO or this repository.

The linked ISO is published independently. The tours show current development and do not certify every beta feature on every machine.
