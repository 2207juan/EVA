# About the desktop tour

Two separately recorded EVA sessions: English interface and voice for English; Spanish interface and voice for Spanish. Each tour is one continuous capture at real speed, with Spanish subtitles and local synthetic narration. No cuts, time compression or inserted UI scenes.

The compositor and shell are real, running in a disposable isolated test session. This is not a physical-installation benchmark. Files and game entries are demonstrations; game covers do not establish that the games run. AI providers are left unconfigured.

## Downloaded demonstration media

- Video wallpaper: **Spinning Earth with clouds, atmosphere, and night lights**, NASA Scientific Visualization Studio; Kel Elkins (USRA), William Putman (NASA/GSFC); Blue Marble data Reto Stockli (NASA/GSFC). [Original and credits](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/5570/). [NASA media guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/). NASA does not endorse EVA.
- Portal 2, Left 4 Dead 2 and Team Fortress 2 cover artwork: Steam / Valve official artwork CDN. Used within the desktop library demonstration; the games are not installed or launched. Artwork remains the property of its owners.
- Voice: local Piper TTS, `en_US-ljspeech-medium` and `es_MX-ald-medium`. Model/dataset provenance is recorded in `assets/tours/CREDITS.txt`.

Demo media and speech models are not added to EVA's ISO. The original EVA video is preserved on the presentation page.
